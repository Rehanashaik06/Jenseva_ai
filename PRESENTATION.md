# PRESENTATION.md — JanSeva AI Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: The Problem

**Title:** Governance Has a Data Blindspot

- MPs receive 400–1000+ citizen complaints every month — zero intelligence layer
- Issues are reported via WhatsApp, paper, calls — no unified, structured system
- Ward-level data is siloed, outdated, and inaccessible to decision-makers
- Officials manually prioritize based on political pressure, not population impact
- Report generation takes days; ministry deadlines are missed
- **Result:** Critical issues like PHC drug shortages and drainage flooding go unaddressed for weeks

- MP Copilot: Conversational AI for instant constituency insights
- One-click monthly report: Ministry-ready PDF in 2 minutes vs. 2 days
- **Tagline:** "From citizen voices to data-backed public action."

## Slide 4: AI Priority Engine

**Title:** Transparent AI — Every Number Has a Reason

**Formula:**
```
Priority Score = (Volume × 0.30) + (Severity × 0.25) + (Population × 0.20) + (Proximity × 0.15) + (Urgency × 0.10)
```

**Score Bands:**
- 90–100: CRITICAL — Immediate action (e.g., Ward 3 Drainage: 94, Ward 7 PHC: 91)
- 75–89: HIGH — Action within 48–72 hours (e.g., Ward 5 Roads: 86)
- 50–74: MEDIUM — Schedule this week (e.g., Ward 9 Waste: 73)
- 0–49: LOW — Plan this month (e.g., Ward 10 Bins: 58)

**Innovation:** Full AI reasoning in plain language for every scored issue
